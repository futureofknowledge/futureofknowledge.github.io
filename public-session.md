## Public session

**Tuesday 17 November 2026, afternoon**
[VENUE TBC], Amsterdam
Free to attend. Registration opens [DATE] at [REGISTRATION LINK].

The second afternoon of the convening is open to anyone: practitioners, policymakers, students, journalists, and members of the public.

### What happens

**Two instruments, shown in public.** The Invisible Language Monitor scores every documented living language on the digital presence it receives relative to the speakers it has. LITMUS asks the next question: whether current language models can read and write those languages at all — the right script, real words, grammatical sentences. Both are open and free to use.

**A panel with partner organisations**, spanning open knowledge infrastructure, frontline translation for asylum seekers, and community language work in South Asia.

**A premiere.** First footage from a film made with the Toto community of Totopara, on their own knowledge of a changing climate. Toto has roughly 1,000 remaining speakers and was encoded for digital use only recently. Children from the community were trained in filmmaking and worked on the recording in their own language.

### Who it is for

Anyone who buys, builds, funds or regulates language technology, and anyone whose work depends on being understood in a language that software handles badly. No technical background is assumed.
