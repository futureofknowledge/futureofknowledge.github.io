## Programme

Sessions run as short provocations followed by small-group work, not as conference talks. Each breakout has a scribe, and what the groups produce feeds directly into the shared statement drafted on day two.

A draft of that statement circulates to all participants in advance. The convening works on an existing text rather than starting one.
